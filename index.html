What is Kudoken?

Kudoken (KDU) is an ERC-20 token on Ethereum with a fixed supply of 1,000,000,000. It was launched in 2021 by Jessie Sponberg and Gabriel Rogers as the unofficial token of galactivism, and it is becoming the shared currency for Jessie's projects.

How do I buy KDU?

Use the Buy KDU button. It opens Uniswap with the real Kudoken contract already selected. Connect an Ethereum wallet, choose how much ETH to swap, and confirm. There is one pool, so check the price impact Uniswap shows you before you confirm.

Can anyone make more Kudokens?

No. The verified contract has no mint function, so the supply is fixed at one billion. It also has no transfer tax and no admin controls.

What does it cost to swap?

The token itself charges nothing. The Uniswap pool has a 0.3% fee that goes to liquidity providers, and Ethereum charges a network gas fee for every transaction.

What can I do with KDU today?

Today you can hold it, send it and trade it. Winning it in Monkey Jockey, spending it at the Outlet Mall or in Biorama, sanctuary staking and business starter tokens are all in development and not live yet.

Is this financial advice?

No. Nothing on this site is financial advice and nobody is promising returns. Crypto prices move and you can lose what you put in. Only use money you are comfortable losing, and do your own research.
