What is Kudoken?

Kudoken (KDU) is an ERC-20 token on Ethereum with a fixed supply of 1,000,000,000, deployed on May 6, 2021 by Jessie Sponberg and Gabriel Rogers. It is the unofficial token of galactivism and the shared currency for Jessie's projects.

How do I buy KDU?

Use any Buy KDU button. It opens Uniswap with the real contract already selected. Connect an Ethereum wallet, choose how much ETH to swap and confirm. There is one pool, so check the price impact Uniswap shows before you confirm.

Why is so much in one wallet?

92.8% of all KDU (928,063,063) is the founders' long-term cold storage. It went in on October 21, 2021 and has not moved since. You can check it yourself on Etherscan.

Can anyone make or destroy KDU?

No. The verified contract has no mint and no burn function, no transfer tax and no admin controls.

What is the move to Base?

A plan, in development, to bridge KDU to Base so everyday payments cost a fraction of a cent. It uses the official Base bridge, so KDU on Base is always matched by KDU locked on Ethereum and the total supply stays the same. It is not live yet.

Is the wallet demo real money?

No. It runs on Base Sepolia, a test network, with test tokens that have no value.

Is this financial advice?

No. Nothing on this site is financial advice and nobody is promising returns. Crypto prices move and you can lose what you put in. Do your own research.
