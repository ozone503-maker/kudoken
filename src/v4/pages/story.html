The long
way round

Nine years, one billion tokens, and a wallet nobody touched. Here is how Kudoken got from a first try to right now.

2017The first try

2017, the first try

Kudoken started as an idea in 2017. We took a first swing at it back then. It was early. We set it down, kept the name, and promised ourselves we would come back to it properly.

Some ideas need
a second run

2021The launch

2021, the launch

On May 6, 2021, Jessie Sponberg and Gabriel Rogers deployed one billion KDU on Ethereum. One time, fixed, with no button anywhere to make more.

On July 15 we opened a Uniswap pool with 3.45 ETH and 125,000,000 KDU. Roughly $10k went in to get it started, and it ran about 7x.

Oct 21October 21, 2021

October 21, 2021, into cold storage

We pulled 53,063,063 KDU and 6.45 ETH back out of the pool and put the stash into cold storage. Then Jessie moved to FlashTown with a five year plan.

The plan was simple to say and hard to do. Leave the tokens alone. Build the places they are meant for first.

Five years
in FlashTown

While the tokens sat still, life in FlashTown kept going, and the places KDU is meant for started to take shape. Slowly, by hand, so that when KDU came back it would have somewhere to go.

Mostly, though, it was patience. Leaving a pile of tokens alone for five years is harder than it sounds.

Days untouched

1,823days

That is how long the cold storage wallet has gone without moving a single token. 928,063,063 KDU, about 92.8% of everything, untouched since October 21, 2021. Nobody touched it. Not once.

The counter updates every time you load this page. You can check the wallet yourself on Etherscan.

October 2026
right on schedule

Five years later, almost to the day, the plan landed. Kudoken is back, this time with somewhere to spend it: games, a mall, a wallet, and a town that has been getting built the whole time.
